The GUI itself was launched once, under a virtual X display
(Xvfb :97, 1600x1000), opening first-light.mscz:

  DISPLAY=:97 ./AppRun first-light.mscz

The screenshot is musescore-lab/gui-first-run.png. What it shows,
verifiable from the image:
  - the fixture's title ("First Light — a short study melody")
    and composer ("The Guide's Lab") typeset at the top,
  - the first-run "Welcome to MuseScore Studio" theme wizard,
  - the Palettes panel: Clefs, Key signatures, Time signatures,
    Tempo, Pitch, Accidentals, Dynamics, Articulations, Text,
    Keyboard, Repeats & jumps, Barlines, Layout,
  - the note-entry toolbar, and the Parts and Mixer buttons,
  - the second system of the score, in G major (one sharp),
    and the final whole note.
The lab used the GUI for this look only. No notes were entered
by hand, and every click-path in the guide's GUI chapters is
labelled documented-only.
