The fixture: musescore-lab/first-light.musicxml — an 8-bar melody
written by hand as MusicXML for this lab (29 notes, a repeat around
bars 1-4, a key change from C major to G major at bar 5, a tempo
marking of 100 BPM, and MIDI program 73 named in the file).

Every conversion below is the same shape:
  mscore4portable -o <output> first-light.musicxml
(run under xvfb-run, HOME pointed at the lab folder; Qt prints a few
lines of locale/dbus warnings on every run — they are noise, and the
real results are the files.)
